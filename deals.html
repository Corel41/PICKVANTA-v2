Deals

A place to see offers attached to products and services. Every deal shows what is being offered, the deal price, the reference price it is measured against, the seller, the location, the validity window and its conditions.

Every offer on this page is invented for demonstration. Nothing is available, claimable or checked against live pricing.

What a deal contains

Deals are modelled as an offer attached to a product or a service — never as a product on its own.

  • Deal price
  • Reference price
  • Discount
  • Validity period
  • Seller
  • Conditions
